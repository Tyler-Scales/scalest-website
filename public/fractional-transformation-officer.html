01 · How it works

Scoped, part-time, and built to hand off.

Role
A senior Scale Street leader serves as your part-time Chief Transformation Officer and owns outcomes alongside your leadership, across all three stages and both threads of the model, with our team behind them.
Scope
Full scope, or one focus area that can expand later.
Time
A set number of days per week, typically 1–2.
Term
Typically 6–12 months; 12 months for full scope.
Agreement
A statement of work, with 60–90 days’ notice to end.
Start
A 30-day diagnostic.
End
A handoff to your internal team.

02 · The 30-day diagnostic

Every engagement starts with a baseline.

The first 30 days answer three questions before any plan is set. The diagnostic sets the baseline that later progress is measured against.

  1. Portfolio

    What is in flight, who owns it, and what it is meant to deliver.

  2. Data

    What you can measure today, and what you can’t.

  3. AI readiness

    Which AI tools are in use or under review, and how they are governed.

04 · Getting started

From first conversation to statement of work.

  1. A first conversation

    You describe what you’re trying to change and where it’s stuck. We tell you whether a fractional role fits, and which scope makes sense.

  2. A short proposal

    Scope (full or focused), days per week, term, and what the diagnostic will cover.

  3. A statement of work

    It sets out:

    • Scope: full, or the focus area(s)
    • Time commitment and term
    • What the 30-day diagnostic delivers
    • How progress will be measured and reported
    • The governance cadence: who your Fractional Chief Transformation Officer reports to, and how often
    • The handoff plan to your internal team
    • Notice to end (60–90 days)
